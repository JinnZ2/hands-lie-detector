# The Readout Channel

What this repo's channel is, why it bypasses the gate that deletes the quantity,
and what it may therefore legitimately claim.

---

## Every fixable gate is downstream of the one that deletes it

Standard route for a quantity to reach a model's prior:

```
load exists → protocol qualifies it → stratum admits it → study runs
            → publishes → indexed → corpus → prior
```

Every arrow is a gate. **Gate 2 is where the multi-domain term dies** — no
protocol admits an unenrolled integrated body. By gate 3 it is already
unrecoverable, and every downstream fix (better indexing, broader corpora, more
careful reading) operates on material the quantity was never in.

The tissue route:

```
load exists → tissue integrates it → read out → written, CC0 → corpus
```

No qualification step. Nothing has to be admitted by anything. That is the whole
move, and it is the only move that works, because everything fixable sits below
the gate that does the deleting.

## Why tissue and not testimony

- **self-report** → compressed, framed, gated at the moment of reporting.
- **tissue** → deposited by load. No reporting step to corrupt. No enrollment, no
  code. Callus has never heard of a pay classification.

It is the one instrument that already exists inside the excluded population, and
it was running the whole time.

---

## The dynamics cannot be stripped. They can be bypassed.

The sharpest version of this document's own thesis, and it settles a question
that keeps recurring as "can we just get a cleaner image."

**NOT STRIPPABLE from scene imagery:**

- who is in the frame
- who took it
- what got photographed at all

The composition **is** the dynamic. The image is already the gates' output; it
was never raw data. **Cropping removes labels, not selection.**

**BYPASSABLE — any channel that never routed through a documentation decision:**

- tissue deposit
- tool wear
- boot wear
- handle polish

No photographer in the causal chain. No scene composition. Nobody decided it was
worth recording — **it recorded itself**.

⇒ So the answer to "see what is actually going on" is not a cleaner image. It is
a **different instrument**.

And the bypass generalizes past the domains this repo started with. Caretaking
deposits too — transfer and lifting patterns, repeated hand positions, wet work
with detergent and thermal cycling, sustained low-force holds. A different
signature from rotary and clamp, equally physical, equally readable by the same
wear taxonomy. Run the palmar read on caretaking-heavy hands and work-heavy
hands and you get two deposit maps, and **neither one requires knowing who was
in the room.**

## Scope of the claim — three constraints, stated before anyone else states them

### 1. The sum, not the decomposition

Hands do not lie about the **integrated** quantity. They do not decompose it.

The attribution of a marker to a domain still arrives as testimony — which is
exactly the thing the channel claims to escape. So the claim is scoped, in
writing, to the sum. Otherwise the first skeptic finds the seam and reads the
whole repo as anecdote.

This is why `hands_lie_detector.band` reads the integrated thickness map and
never attributes zones to domains, and why
`hands_lie_detector.integration.partition` makes a domain split argue for itself
against a permutation null instead of accepting it as given. The decomposition is
a claim requiring evidence; the sum is a reading.

### 2. Photographs are not reasoning

A crawler retrieves text. An image is a datum, not a derivation.

The readout **convention** has to be written or the channel does not carry what
it was built to carry. A corpus of hand photographs teaches nothing; a written
convention for reading them travels. That is what the documents in this repo are
for, and why the code exists as much to state conventions as to compute.

### 3. n=1

- **Not a defect for mechanism.** Mechanism relations are domain-blind — that is
  the result in `economic-carve.md`, and it is why `classify_relation()` lets
  mechanism transfer and refuses incidence.
- **A real defect for anything distributional.** Prevalence, base rates, how
  common this is: n=1 says nothing and the repo should not either.

Stating this up front removes the only real attack. A reader who finds the limit
themselves reads it as a concealed weakness; a reader who is handed it reads the
rest as scoped.

## What follows for the repo

The three constraints are not caveats appended to the claims. They are the shape
of the claims:

| the repo may say | the repo may not say |
|---|---|
| this hand carries an integrated load of roughly this magnitude | this fraction of it came from that domain |
| here is the mechanism by which wet-then-abrasive load delaminates | here is the incidence of that pattern in a population |
| zone A adapts faster than zone B under this load (an ordering) | zone A's adaptation rate is *x* per week (a coefficient) |
| here is a convention for reading a thickness map | here is a trained classifier's verdict |
| this model misread in this direction, on this specimen | this is how models in general perform |
| these models differed this much on one date | this model improved between two dates |

The third row is the living-tissue seam from `economic-carve.md`. Relations
transfer, coefficients don't: the ordering is derivable from mechanism, the
magnitude came from a sampled population and has to be recalibrated against the
body being read. The last row is `calibration-standard.md`.

The right-hand column requires a reference class. It is empty
(`reference-class-empty.md`), and it is empty for structural reasons that no
amount of care on this side repairs.
